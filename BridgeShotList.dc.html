Rules that hold across all thirty-three

  • Full colour throughout — no duotone, no filters, no black and white.
  • One grey for every garment ground, so the listing grids sit flat.
  • Riding shots get natural light only; the deep blue must stay true against a bright sky.
  • No text, logos or graphic overlay baked into any file — the layout supplies all type.
  • Crop to the stated ratio before supplying: slots fill and crop from the centre.

Where the flow forks

Nav search and sign-in leave the flow to their own screens; the bag icon jumps straight to checkout from any screen. The product page also offers a direct route to checkout beside add-to-bag.

Mobile differences

Filters collapse into a sheet, the two-column splits stack, tap targets rise to 48px and headline sizes drop a step. Same screens, same order — no separate mobile path.

Validation

Checkout blocks on a malformed email with an inline error alert, then shows a taking-payment state before confirmation. Everything else is optimistic.

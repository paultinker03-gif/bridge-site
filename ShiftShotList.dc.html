SHIFT — photography brief

SHIFT shot list

SHIFT runs on the same design system as Bridge but not on the same photography. Bridge shoots the product as it leaves the factory: clean ground, even light, nothing in the way. SHIFT shoots the product as it comes back — worn, mended, labelled, on a bench. The difference in the pictures is what makes the two houses read apart when the type and the grid are shared.

Eleven of these positions are currently filled by re-used Bridge files (marked placeholder below) — they work, but they are the factory version of the garment and undercut the argument. The eighteen shots listed here replace them.

The look

Light

Available daylight, north-facing. No fill, no flash, no beauty dish. Shadows are allowed to land where they land.

Ground

Real surfaces: unbleached paper, steel bench, painted brick, a plywood offcut. Never a seamless sweep.

Condition

Show the wear. Fade, a darned elbow, a replacement zip in a slightly different black. Mends are in focus, not hidden.

People

Hands, not faces. The bench staff at work; the rider is implied by the garment, not modelled.

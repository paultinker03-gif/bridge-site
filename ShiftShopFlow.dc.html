Where the flow forks

Search opens inline above the grid rather than on its own screen. Trade-in and Your record leave the purchase path; the bag jumps to checkout from anywhere.

Mobile differences

Filters collapse into a sheet, the two-column splits stack, tap targets rise to 48px and headline sizes drop a step. Same screens, same order — no separate mobile path.

Validation

Single-unit stock means checkout is time-sensitive: a malformed email blocks with an inline alert, then a taking-payment state before the piece is released.
